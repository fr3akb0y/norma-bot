# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how this Discord bot processes information.

## Data Collection

The bot may temporarily process information provided by Discord that is necessary to execute its functionality, including:

- Discord user IDs
- Discord server IDs
- Discord channel IDs
- Command input
- Message-related information when required for a command

The bot does not intentionally collect or permanently store personal information unless this is required for a specific feature.

## Commands

When a user executes a command, Discord may provide information about the user, server, channel, and command to the bot.

This information is processed only as necessary to execute the requested command.

## Webhooks

The bot may use Discord webhooks for certain functionality.

When webhooks are used, information necessary to send or process the webhook may be transmitted through Discord's services.

## Data Sharing

Data processed by the bot is not sold or used for advertising.

Information may be processed by Discord as part of the normal operation of Discord and its API.

## Data Retention

Unless explicitly required by a feature, the bot does not permanently store Discord user information.

Temporary logs may be created for debugging and operation of the bot.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of the bot changes.

## Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact the bot owner through Discord or the GitHub repository associated with this bot.
